Manual inspection logic

01 — Lid pressAn operator presses the lid as the jar reaches their station.
02 — Acoustic / tactile indicationIf a “BOO” popping response is observed, the jar is flagged as potentially defective. This is a screening assumption, not a validated leak test.
03 — Manual sortingFlagged jars are sent to the reject area; jars that pass continue downstream. Human accuracy, fatigue and throughput still require testing.
